# Engagement Principles (V2)

## Goal
Increase repeat usage by delivering consistent utility and positive feedback loops.

## Allowed patterns
- Fast first success in core flows.
- Clear progress indicators.
- Helpful reminders with explicit user control.
- Personalization based on user-owned settings and behavior.
- Personalized quick actions on home.

## Disallowed patterns (no dark patterns)
- Misleading prompts.
- Forced opt-ins hidden behind confusing copy.
- Manipulative countdowns or fake scarcity.
- Notification spam without clear value.

## Metric linkage
- D1 and D7 retention.
- Task completion and abandonment rates.
- User-controlled notification opt-in rates.
